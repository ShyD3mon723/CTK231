My name is Alexander Cantu (I go by Alex) and I am a Hispanic artist that loves the crossroads of horror art and psychology. Making monsters either that will scare people or make them feel uneasy, or taking those feelings and turning them into monsters is the art I love to do! While I do enjoy the dark and gritty, I also love the opposite end of the spectrum with all things cute and fuzzy. I enjoy sometimes trying to cross these opposites and make adorable monsters.

I was born in Aurora Illinois and stayed in this state my entire life thus far. My parents separated when I was three. I was raised by my loving mother who has encouraged my art from the beginning. I also watched my uncle, who draws for Marvel comics and worked on Spawn, draw and was inspired by seeing him work. Because of them I have strived to be an artist for as long as I can remember. I would see my father every other weekend on which I would endure many forms of abuse, these horrific actions sparked a darker side to my art as a healthier way of processing emotions. When I would turn 12 my father would later commit suicide which would drive me to be hospitalized for a mental breakdown. I would stay at a mental hospital 3 times in total for being so overwhelmed by grief and pain that I could not begin to process in a healthy manner. Once I got better control of myself, I would decide that I want to spread awareness of mental illness through my art.

I moved from Aurora IL to Decatur IL to be with my now husband. He helped me figure out college and I would enroll and Richland Community College. I managed to keep a perfect GPA the entire time I was there. I transferred to ISU in Bloomington to pursue a creative technologies degree to get into the gaming industry. As I write this, I am still working on my degree and will have to update this as I progress through life more.
